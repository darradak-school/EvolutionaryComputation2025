EXERCISE 5: CUSTOM ACO ALGORITHM ANALYSIS - Darcy Lisk (a1850193)

=== Algorithm Overview ===
My implementation of a custom ACO algorithm uses 15 ants per iteration with an elitist pheromone 
update strategy and optional local search. 

=== Performance / Comparison with MMAS/MMAS* ===
Performance: On unimodal functions (F1-F3), local search provides significant advantage seen in 
the rapid improvement on comparison plots. On complex landscapes (F18, F23-F25), MMAS/MMAS* may 
perform betterdue to their carefully tuned parameter strategies for specific problem structures, 
our specific ACO implementation outperformed MMAS/MMAS* on F24 whilst underperforming in F18, 
F23 and F25.
