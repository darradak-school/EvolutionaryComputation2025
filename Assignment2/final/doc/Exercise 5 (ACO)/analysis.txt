=== Performance / Comparison with MMAS/MMAS* ===
Performance: On unimodal functions (F1-F3), local search provides significant advantage seen in 
the rapid improvement on comparison plots. On complex landscapes (F18, F23-F25), MMAS/MMAS* may 
perform betterdue to their carefully tuned parameter strategies for specific problem structures, 
our specific ACO implementation outperformed MMAS/MMAS* on F24 whilst underperforming in F18, 
F23 and F25.

summary:

On unimodal functions (F1–F3), both RLS and the (1+1) EA demonstrate clear advantages through rapid 
and consistent convergence, outperforming Random Search by effectively exploiting the fitness gradient. 
On more complex, epistatic landscapes (F18, F23–F25), performance differences become more nuanced. 
While Random Search consistently underperforms due to its lack of exploitation, EA and RLS show varying 
strengths depending on the problem structure. EA’s probabilistic mutation enables broader exploration, 
giving it an edge on rugged landscapes like F25, whereas RLS excels in tightly structured problems such as F24
 due to its precise, single-bit mutation.
